Introduction & Background

  • Provenance — a concept borrowed from art history and archival science — is now central to AI authorship debates.
  • Policy is moving fast: the EU AI Act, US Copyright Office guidance, and India's 2022 Amendment all now address AI-generated works.
  • Cases like Zhou v. OpenAI (2025) and Raman v. Infosys (2026) are shaping judicial expectations for authorship evidence.

Slide 4

Objectives of the Study

01

Design a provenance schema recording prompts, model parameters, and outputs.

02

Build a working prototype and test it across multiple generative media types.

03

Measure impact on attribution accuracy and copyright confidence, and shape policy recommendations.

Slide 5

Review of Literature

  • Ownership models range from hybrid developer-user rights (Wangmo) to prompt-based ownership (Shafiq).
  • Technical approaches embed traceable metadata directly during generation (Khan et al.), reducing disputes in co-created work (Lauk-Dubitskiy).
  • Scholars increasingly call for a codified "machine-assisted author" category (Oliveira) with contribution-based thresholds (Ramos-Zaga).

Slide 6

Methodology

Design & Sample

Empirical, descriptive study; five-point Likert questionnaire; 210 participants via convenience sampling.

Analysis

SPSS v21 — chi-square tests, one-way ANOVA, and Pearson correlation.

Variables

Independent: age, gender, education, area, occupation. Dependent: awareness and support.

Slide 8

Hypothesis Testing

  • All 5 chi-square tests (age, gender, education, employment, area) returned p > 0.05.
  • All 3 ANOVA tests (age group, education, occupation) also returned p > 0.05.
  • No demographic variable showed a statistically significant association at the 5% level (n = 210).

Slide 9

Discussion & Limitations

  • Older, more educated, and urban respondents were consistently more sceptical of AI-tracking claims and current oversight.
  • Convenience sampling and self-reported measures may limit generalisability and introduce response bias.
  • A cross-sectional design means the findings can't establish causal change in attitudes over time.

Slide 10

Conclusion & Recommendations

  • Provenance tracking is feasible and welcomed by a majority of the creators surveyed.
  • Harmonise international standards and build lightweight, interoperable provenance modules into AI platforms.
  • Run co-design workshops with creators and fund open-source provenance infrastructure with ongoing monitoring.
