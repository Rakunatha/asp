Abstract

Judicial review of intellectual property rights decisions is a crucial aspect of the Indian legal system. It ensures that decisions made by administrative tribunals are fair and just, and the judiciary plays a vital role in interpreting, defending, and applying the law.

The study aims to examine the effectiveness of judicial review of administrative tribunals in India, particularly in the context of IPR decisions. The sample size is 230 respondents across multiple demographic categories.

Introduction & Objectives

IPR disputes in India were adjudicated by the Intellectual Property Appellate Board (IPAB) until its abolition in 2021, after which functions were transferred to High Courts. Judicial review under Articles 32 & 226 of the Constitution safeguards against arbitrariness and ensures the rule of law.

OBJECTIVE 01

Examine the current judicial review process of administrative tribunals in India in the context of IPR decisions.

OBJECTIVE 02

Analyse how judicial review ensures tribunals make decisions aligned with the law without infringing individual rights.

OBJECTIVE 03

Identify strengths and weaknesses of the current process and suggest areas for improvement.

OBJECTIVE 04

Investigate the impact of judicial review on tribunal functioning and its implications for the Indian legal system.

Conclusion & Policy Implications

This study critically examines judicial review of IPR decisions rendered by administrative tribunals in India. The abolition of IPAB in 2021 has intensified the discourse on the role and scope of judicial review, with High Courts now balancing deference to technical expertise with constitutional principles.

The research seeks to contribute to a nuanced understanding of the relationship between administrative adjudication and constitutional control in the field of intellectual property law.

Policy Recommendation: Legislative reforms are necessary to strengthen the judicial review process and ensure that administrative tribunals are held accountable — ultimately leading to a more just and fair society.
