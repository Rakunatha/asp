Public Opinion Towards the Impact of School Education in Chennai

n = 223 respondents Method: Empirical · Convenient Sampling Frame: Chennai — City, Town & Village Tool: SPSS · Bar Graph Analysis
Introduction

Understanding the State of
School Education in India

Indian education is a diverse landscape that combines traditional wisdom with modern learning — spanning a wide spectrum from ancient gurukuls to cutting-edge technology-driven institutions. Challenges like access and quality persist, but India's commitment to education remains strong, fostering a new generation poised to lead in a globalized world.

This research investigates public opinion regarding the effectiveness and impact of school education in Chennai, India. Data was collected through a comprehensive survey administered to a diverse sample of Chennai residents, comprising parents, students, educators, and community members.

The study reveals a range of opinions, with a significant portion of respondents expressing concerns about the need to improve teacher-student ratio, more extracurricular activities, implement professionalism and more opportunities based on student's skills in Chennai schools. The study concludes that level of educational qualification and marital status impacts perceptions of school education in Chennai.

Discussion

Key Findings from the Study

👥

Age & Teacher Perception

Respondents in the 20–30 age group believe teacher harassment is a consequence of modern school education, while those under 20 also identify effective communication as a result of contemporary schooling.

📐

Teacher–Student Ratio

Both age groups advocate for improving the teacher-student ratio. Those aged 20–30 are highly supportive, while under-20 respondents favour the enhancement too, suggesting broad consensus.

🎭

Extracurricular Activities

Respondents under 20 strongly agree that extracurricular activities influence students positively. Those aged 20–30 hold a general agreement on their impact on student development.

🎓

Education Level Matters

Professionals and well-educated respondents are more aware of both the pros and cons of present schooling and its impact on a child's life than those with less educational background.

⚖️

Marital Status & Views

Marital status was found to impact perceptions of school education in Chennai, suggesting that life stage and family responsibilities shape opinions on the education system.

🔗

Positive Correlation

A positive correlation exists among factors acting as benefits of schooling and the negative aspects simultaneously — respondents who see more benefits also recognise more drawbacks.

Conclusion

Bridging the Gap in School Education

It is deduced from the study that individuals' age groups influence their opinions on the quality and effectiveness of school education. There is a gender-based difference in public opinion regarding school education. The level of educational qualification influences respondents' views on the education system.

The marital status also impacts perceptions of school education in Chennai. The professionals and well-educated respondents are more aware of the pros and cons of present schooling and its impact on the life of a child than those with less education.

In order to bridge the existing gap, measures should be taken to educate and provide awareness about proper schooling. Policies should be framed to increase and encourage a holistic approach in schools.

RIGHT TO EDUCATION ACT

India's Legal Framework for Quality Education

The RTE Act plays a pivotal role in India's educational landscape, providing a legal framework for the realization of every child's right to quality education. Its provisions cover a wide spectrum — from teacher deployment to curriculum development — all with the aim of fostering equitable and inclusive education.

The RTE Act stands as a testament to India's commitment to providing accessible and high-quality education to its youth, reflecting a vision of a brighter future for all.
