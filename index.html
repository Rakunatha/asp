Abstract & Overview

Judicial review of IPR decisions is crucial in India's legal system. Constitutional courts under Articles 32 & 226 ensure administrative tribunals act fairly, lawfully, and within procedural propriety.

Judicial Review IPR Admin Tribunals India IPAB High Courts

Intellectual Property Rights have emerged as a critical component of modern economic and technological development. Administrative bodies like the erstwhile IPAB adjudicated IPR disputes — but following the abolition of IPAB in 2021, functions transferred to High Courts.

Judicial review acts as a safeguard against arbitrariness, jurisdictional errors, and natural justice violations, maintaining the rule of law in administrative decision-making.

Research Objectives

01

Examine Current Process

Analyse the existing judicial review process of administrative tribunals in India, specifically in the context of intellectual property rights decisions.

02

Role of Judicial Review

Evaluate how judicial review ensures that administrative tribunals make decisions aligned with the law without infringing on individual rights.

03

Strengths & Weaknesses

Identify gaps and strengths in the current process, and recommend targeted areas for reform and legislative improvement.

04

Systemic Impact

Investigate the broader impact of judicial review on tribunal functioning and the long-term implications for the Indian legal system.

Conclusion & Policy Implications

This study critically examines how judicial review of IPR decisions rendered by administrative tribunals in India operates within a constitutional framework. The abolition of IPAB in 2021 has intensified the discourse on the role and scope of judicial review, with High Courts now balancing deference to technical expertise against constitutional principles.

Findings suggest that legislative reforms are urgently needed to strengthen the judicial review process, ensure tribunal accountability, and ultimately achieve a more just and fair society for IP rights holders and innovators.
